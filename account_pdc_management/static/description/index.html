Post Dated Cheque Management

Hold a post dated cheque in its own account until the bank decides its fate — then clear it, bounce it, redeposit it, and recover the bank’s return fee from the partner, every step posted as an ordinary journal entry.

Feature overview

Everything is driven from a normal customer or vendor payment and configured under Accounting › Configuration — one tick box on the journal, one holding account, and five settings.

Capture the cheque

  • Flag any journal as a post dated cheque journal.
  • Give that journal its own PDC holding account.
  • Cheque number, cheque date and drawee bank on every payment.
  • Cheques never inflate the bank balance before they clear.
  • Works for customer cheques and vendor cheques alike.

Clear, bounce, redeposit

  • Clear a cheque into the bank account you actually deposited it in.
  • Clear on the date the bank honoured it, not the date you noticed.
  • Bounce puts the balance straight back onto the partner.
  • Return reason and bank return advice kept on the record.
  • Redeposit a bounced cheque, and bounce it again if it fails twice.
  • Full history on the cheque, each step linked to the entry it posted.

Recover the bank charge

  • Return fee booked as Dr Bank Charges / Cr Bank when it is incurred.
  • Bill the fee back with a bank recovery invoice.
  • Invoice hard-capped at the fee you actually paid.
  • Waive part of the fee and the invoice drops accordingly.
  • Waive all of it and no invoice is raised at all.
  • Recovery taxes and sales journal chosen in settings.

What’s included
