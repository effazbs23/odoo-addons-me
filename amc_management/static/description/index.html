AMC Management

Annual maintenance contracts that accrue month by month, prove every service visit, and bill the vendor against the provision they earned.

Feature overview

A lift contract, a chiller contract, a generator contract — each signed once and consumed all year. AMC Management turns the purchase order into a tracked contract with its own service calendar, its own accrual entries and its own milestone bills, all configured from the order you already raise.

Contracts & service periods

  • Flag a purchase order as an AMC order; its lines then accept service products only.
  • Mark as Signed raises one Mother AMC per order line.
  • Each contract is cut into service periods by the chosen frequency, on the calendar where the dates allow it and evenly by days where they do not.
  • Every contract and period carries a traceable reference, right down to PO-1-P3.
  • Sites, contractors and product categories carried through from the order.

Accrual accounting

  • Monthly draft provisions per order: Dr AMC Expense / Cr AMC Provision.
  • Catch-up folding for a contract signed late, so no month is missed.
  • Expense and provision accounts resolved per product category, so one order can cover sites booked to different accounts.
  • Unserviced days are reversed out of the provision with their own draft entry.
  • Analytic distribution stamped per site on every line.

Billing & reporting

  • Milestone vendor bills driven by the order's payment term.
  • Sequential-billing control: Q3 cannot be raised while Q2 is still in draft.
  • Each bill records the quarter it is for and the service periods it covers.
  • Service reports copied onto the bill, signed contract attached automatically.
  • AMC Schedule report in XLSX: a twelve-month provision grid per contract and year.
