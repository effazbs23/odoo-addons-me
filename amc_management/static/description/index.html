AMC Management

Annual maintenance contracts that accrue month by month, prove every service visit, and bill the vendor against the provision they earned.

One invoice in January, twelve months of service

A lift contract, a chiller contract, a generator contract — each is signed once and consumed all year. Booked the standard way, the whole cost lands in one month, the other eleven look free, and nothing in the system records whether the vendor actually turned up in May.

Without this module
  • The full contract value hits a single month.
  • Service visits live in e-mail, not in Odoo.
  • A missed visit is still paid in full.
  • Quarterly vendor bills are typed by hand and drift out of sequence.
  • No one can answer “how much AMC is accrued so far?”
With AMC Management
  • The cost is spread across the months it covers, automatically.
  • Every service period is a record, with a mandatory service report.
  • Unserviced days are reversed out of the provision.
  • Milestone bills follow the order's payment term and refuse to skip a step.
  • A twelve-month provision grid is one click away in Excel.

The whole flow, five steps

Nothing to learn beyond the purchase order you already use.

Step 1

Tick “AMC Order” on the request for quotation

The AMC block appears on the order header: site, start date, duration and how many service periods the contract is cut into. From that moment the order accepts service products only — enforced by the product domain in the view and by a Python constraint, so nothing storable slips onto a maintenance contract.

Step 2

Confirm — one contract per line, cut into service periods

Each order line becomes a Mother AMC, split into the number of service periods you asked for: four for quarterly servicing, twelve for monthly. A contract spanning whole months is cut on the calendar (15 Jan → 14 Feb); anything else is split evenly by days, with the remainder handed to the earliest periods so the parts always add back up to the whole.

Step 3

Press “Mark as Signed” — the provisions write themselves

One button on the purchase order signs every contract it carries and generates the monthly provision entries in draft, one entry per order per month, with one debit and one credit line per site and the site's analytic account already stamped on them. Signed the contract three months late? The months already gone are folded into a single catch-up entry, so the accrual is never short.

Step 4

Close each period: Done, Partially Serviced or Expired

A service report is mandatory to mark a period done, and a reason is mandatory to expire one. Partially Serviced asks how many days were actually covered and books a draft reversal entry for the rest; Expired reverses the whole period. Existing provision entries are never edited — the correction is always a separate, reviewable entry.

Step 5

Bill the vendor, milestone by milestone

The order's payment term decides how many bills the contract splits into and how much each one carries. Milestone Q2 cannot be raised before Q1 has left draft, and the same milestone cannot be billed twice. The bill line is posted against the provision account, drawing down what was accrued, and the selected periods' service reports plus the signed contract are copied onto it as attachments.

Everything in the box

Service products only

AMC orders reject storable products in the view and in a constraint, so a maintenance contract can never be mixed with goods.

One entry per order per month

Guaranteed by a partial unique index in the database, not by convention. An order covering four sites still yields one consolidated entry per month.

Sites with analytic accounts

Buildings, plants, floors or data centres. Each site carries a code and an analytic account, stamped on every provision and reversal line raised for it.

Sequential milestone billing

Q2 waits for Q1 to leave draft, a milestone cannot be billed twice, and a payment term with four installments allows exactly four bills.

Documents that follow the money

Service reports are copied from the periods onto the bill, along with the signed contract — the originals stay on the period, so deleting a bill takes nothing away.

Expiry reminders

A scheduled action mails the contractor a configurable number of days before a contract ends, and keeps the days-to-expiry counter current.

AMC Schedule in Excel

A twelve-month provision grid per contract and year, with days, contract value, provision to date and provision required — and no third-party report library to install.

Two roles, no accounting rights needed

Users close service periods; managers configure sites, terminate contracts and raise bills. Neither needs access to the accounting app for the entries to be written.

Multi-company & multi-currency

Record rules per company, and a foreign-currency order is converted to company currency at the order date before it is provisioned.

Questions people ask first

Are the journal entries posted automatically?

No. Every provision and reversal entry is created in draft. Your accountant reviews and posts them like any other entry.

What happens if we sign the contract months after it started?

The elapsed months are folded into a single catch-up entry dated in the current month, and the remaining months are provisioned normally from there.

Can one order cover several buildings?

Yes. One order line per site gives one Mother AMC per site, and the monthly provision consolidates them into a single entry carrying one debit and one credit line per site.

Does the person closing a service period need accounting rights?

No. The AMC roles drive the workflow; the accounting side is written for them, and the entries land with Finance for review.

Can we cancel an order after signing?

Not silently. An order whose AMCs are signed refuses to be cancelled or reset to draft until the contracts are terminated and their entries handled — provisions exist by then.

Does it work with an existing chart of accounts?

Yes. You choose the expense and provision accounts; the module creates none of its own and localisations are untouched.
