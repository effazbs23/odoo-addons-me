Feature overview

Everything an auto repair workshop needs to manage operations, technicians, inventory, warranties, and billing smoothly in Odoo.

Job-Card & Workflow

  • 9-state repair order lifecycle from Draft to Invoiced & Closed
  • Labor tracking per technician with service catalog lookups
  • Real stock moves for consumed parts with lot/serial linkage
  • Dual-mode negotiated pricing (percentage or fixed price)
  • Server-side salesperson discount caps strictly enforced
  • Automatic sale order generation and invoice PDF email delivery

Vehicle & Warranty

  • Customer vehicle registry with VIN, license plate, and fuel type
  • Automatic service history records generated on order closure
  • Per-part warranty duration tracking with start/end date calculation
  • Dedicated customer warranty claim review and approval flow
  • Automatic warning alert when a claim is outside warranty period
  • Contextual resolution repair orders linked to original claims

Capacity & Safeguards

  • Realistic pickup estimation using working hours & calendar leaves
  • Workshop bay and lift tracking with concurrent capacity bounds
  • Immutable check-in/check-out inspections with customer signatures
  • Itemized customer belongings return verification checklist
  • Technician performance tracking and employee KPI statistics
  • Manager-approval deletion safeguard with complete audit trail
