User Manual

CRM Win Rate & Lost Reason Analytics

A step-by-step guide to what this module adds to CRM: structured lost-reason categories, mandatory reason capture on Mark Lost, and a single-page visual Win Rate Dashboard.

1. Categorize your lost reasons

CRM → Configuration → Lost Reasons now has a Category column: Pricing, Timing, Competitor, No Budget, No Response, Not a Fit, or Other. Odoo's three default reasons ship pre-categorized; anything you add yourself defaults to "Other" until you set it, so nothing is ever left blank.

2. A reason is now required to mark a lead lost

Click Lost on any lead and the same wizard you already know opens — but "Mark as Lost" is blocked until a reason is selected. This is enforced natively (the field is simply required), so it works whether you're at the kanban "Lost" button or the form's "Lost" button.

3. Two separate notes: Closing Note and Lost Note

The native Closing Note is posted to the lead's chatter, same as always. The new Lost Note below it is a separate, optional field stored directly on the lead — it's what shows up in drill-down views on the dashboard, and it's never used for aggregation, just context for whoever reviews it later.

4. The result: a lead with a categorized, traceable loss

Once confirmed, the lead is marked Lost with the reason recorded on the record and the closing note logged to the chatter. Reopening a lost lead (Restore) automatically clears the reason, category, and lost note — nothing stale carries forward if the deal comes back to life.

5. Win Rate Dashboard: CRM → Reporting → Win Rate Dashboard

One page, everything a sales manager checks weekly: win rate, won/lost deals (each with a vs-previous-period trend), which reason category dominates your losses, win rate by salesperson, where deals drop off in the pipeline, and where your leads are coming from. Filter by date range, team, or salesperson from the dropdowns at top right — the whole page re-fetches instantly. The previous native pivot/graph views are still there under "Win Rate (Pivot)", "Loss Reasons", and "Stage Drop-off" for raw data export or custom grouping.
