Omnichannel Net Profitability Dashboard

Consolidate multi-channel e-commerce sales against true COGS, dynamic ad spends, and channel commissions to reveal actual net profitability in real time.

Overview

The Omnichannel Net Profitability Dashboard gives e-commerce operators a single pane of glass over every sales channel — Shopify, Amazon, WooCommerce, and POS — computing true net margin after COGS, marketplace commissions, advertising spend, and fulfilled shipping costs. Built as an OWL frontend on top of a dedicated abstract engine, it replaces spreadsheet-driven profit reconciliation with live, order-level granularity. Finance teams gain instant visibility into which channels, campaigns, and products actually generate profit — not just revenue.

Highlights

Real-Time Net Margin

Live computation of gross revenue, COGS, fees, ad spend, shipping, and net profit across all confirmed orders.

Multi-Channel Tagging

Automatically classifies every sale by channel — Shopify, Amazon, WooCommerce, or POS — for per-channel profit segmentation.

Ad Spend Attribution

Logs PPC, social, and affiliate ad spend directly on each order for granular customer acquisition cost analysis.

Commission Fee Tracking

Captures per-order marketplace and platform commission fees as a dedicated monetary field on the sales order.

Actual Shipping Costs

Records true fulfilled shipping cost per order, distinct from invoiced freight, for accurate margin calculation.

KPI Card Dashboard

OWL-based frontend renders three top-line KPI cards: Gross Sales, Drain, and Net Profit with margin percentage.

Features

01
Multi-Channel Order Tagging
Classifies every sale.order by channel — Shopify, Amazon, WooCommerce, or POS — enabling per-channel profit segmentation.
02
Commission Fee Tracking
Captures per-order marketplace and platform commission fees as a dedicated monetary field on the sales order.
03
Marketing Spend Attribution
Logs ad spend (PPC, social, affiliates) directly on each order for granular customer acquisition cost analysis.
04
Actual Shipping Cost Logging
Records true fulfilled shipping cost per order, distinct from invoiced freight, for accurate margin calculation.
05
Net Margin Engine
Abstract model (bs.ecom.profit.engine) computes gross revenue, COGS, total channel fees, ad spend, shipping, net profit, and margin ratio across all confirmed/done orders.
06
KPI Card Dashboard UI
OWL-based frontend renders three top-line KPI cards: Gross Sales, Drain (COGS + Fees), and Net Profit with margin percentage.
07
Sale Order Form Extension
Inherits the standard sale.order form to inject a "BS Channel Info" group with channel, commissions, ad spend, and shipping fields.
08
Top-Level Menu
Adds a dedicated "Net Profit Dashboard" application menu with a "Profitability Overview" sub-menu launching the dashboard client action.
09
Loading State Indicator
Displays a spinner with "Calculating..." text while the backend engine aggregates data.
