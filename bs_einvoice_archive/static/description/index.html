E-Invoice Retention & Audit Trail

Capture immutable e-invoice snapshots on posting, enforce legal retention policies with automatic disposal eligibility, track all access in an append-only audit trail, and export audit-ready ZIP bundles with optional asynchronous Google Drive backup.

Feature overview

Everything below ships with E-Invoice Retention & Audit Trail — accessible directly from Accounting and Settings.

Archive & Integrity

  • Auto-archive on invoice post
  • Append-only snapshot records
  • SHA-256 cryptographic checksums
  • Attached PDF and XML preservation
  • Invoice-type classifications
  • Credit & debit note linking

Retention & Policies

  • Company-scoped retention rules
  • Global policy default fallbacks
  • Daily health check cron scan
  • Disposal eligibility promotion
  • Admin-only manual disposal
  • Strict uninstall protection

Audit & Cloud Backup

  • Append-only audit access log
  • Audit-ready ZIP bundle export
  • manifest.json & audit_log.csv
  • Google Drive OAuth per company
  • Fernet encrypted refresh tokens
  • Asynchronous 5-min upload queue

What's included

Immutable snapshot records with SHA-256 integrity check

Attachments preserved on invoice to survive uninstall

Configurable retention policies per invoice type and company

Daily automated health check cron flagging gaps & missing files

Full audit log recording views, exports, and disposal events

Bulk ZIP exporter with manifest.json and audit_log.csv

Optional Google Drive backup with encrypted OAuth credentials

Exponential backoff retry queue & manual retry action

Dependencies

Invoicing (account) Google Account (google_account)
