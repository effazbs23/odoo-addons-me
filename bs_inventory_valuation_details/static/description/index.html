Inventory Valuation Details

View product-level stock valuation, cost method, unit cost, total value, and expected accounting impact directly from transfers, scrap orders, and manufacturing orders.

LGPL-3 Depends: stock_account Depends: mrp_account by ERP23
Transfer Valuation Adds valuation smart buttons on completed receipts, deliveries, returns, dropship moves, and internal transfers.
Expected Journal Entry Shows expected debit and credit accounts based on Odoo 19 stock valuation, stock variation, expense, and WIP accounts.
MRP and Scrap Support Review finished product value, component cost, and scrap valuation from the source documents.

Overview

Inventory Valuation Details gives inventory and accounting users a clear breakdown of the valuation behind completed stock operations. Instead of checking stock moves manually, users can open a valuation view from the related transfer, scrap order, or manufacturing order.

The module uses Odoo's stock accounting data and product configuration to show quantity, unit cost, total valuation, cost method, valuation method, source document, and expected journal lines for operational review.

What It Shows

  • Valuation total on completed stock transfers
  • Valuation total on completed scrap orders
  • Finished product and component valuation on manufacturing orders
  • Quantity, unit cost, total valuation, and product category
  • FIFO, AVCO, and standard cost method display
  • Periodic and perpetual valuation method display
  • Expected debit and credit accounts when no per-move entry exists

Designed For

  • Inventory managers reviewing stock movement cost
  • Accountants validating expected stock valuation impact
  • Manufacturing teams reviewing component and finished goods values
  • Operations teams checking valuation on receipts and deliveries
  • Companies using Odoo 19 stock accounting with MRP

Supported Documents

Transfers Receipts, deliveries, returns, dropship, and internal operations
Manufacturing Finished product and component stock move valuation
Scrap Orders Scrapped product value with source move details
Journal Entries Linked accounting entries where Odoo creates them

Installation

  1. Download the module from the Odoo App Store.
  2. Place the bs_inventory_valuation_details folder in your Odoo addons directory.
  3. Restart the Odoo server.
  4. Go to Settings → Apps, search for Inventory Valuation Details, and click Install.
  5. Open a completed transfer, scrap order, or manufacturing order and use the valuation smart button.
Requirements: Odoo 19.0 with the standard stock_account and mrp_account modules installed.
