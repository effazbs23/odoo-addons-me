Petty Cash Management

Complete Petty Cash Management with Accounting Integration

Overview

The Petty Cash Management module provides a complete solution for managing petty cash operations within Odoo 19. Streamline the entire lifecycle from cash requests and advance payments to settlements and full accounting integration. Empower your employees to submit cash requests through a dedicated portal, track advances, settle expenses with tagged bills, and maintain complete financial control with an interactive dashboard and real-time analytics.

Key Features

Cash Request Workflow:
Employees submit petty cash requests with purpose lines, attachments, and cost center allocations through an intuitive portal or backend interface.
Advance Payment Management:
Issue advances to employees against approved requests, track signed advance documents, and manage the full lifecycle from approval to payment with automated journal entries.
Flexible Settlement Modes:
Settle advances with partial settlement, full settlement, or return of unused advance. Settle directly against vendor bills without an advance when needed.
Full Accounting Integration:
Automatic journal entries for advances, settlements, and cash adjustments. Seamless integration with Odoo Accounting and HR Expense modules.
Interactive Dashboard:
Real-time KPIs, advance type distribution pie chart, monthly trend analysis, top outstanding employees tracking, and request status funnel with drill-down capabilities.
Employee Portal:
Dedicated portal for employees to create, submit, track, and manage their cash requests online with file uploads and multi-line purpose entries.
Cost Center & Vessel Tracking:
Link every transaction to cost centers or vessels via analytic accounts for granular expense allocation and reporting.
Multi-Company & Security Control:
Full multi-company support with company-specific journals, accounts, cost center configurations, and role-based access security.
