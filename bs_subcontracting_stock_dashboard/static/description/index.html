Subcontractor-Owned Stock Dashboard

Clear reporting split between company-owned and subcontractor-owned inventory sitting in the same subcontracting location. Smarter reorder rules, better stock levels, lower costs.

Feature overview

Reads your existing subcontracting locations and stock.quant ownership as-is — no new stock-move logic, purely aggregation and presentation.

Ownership Visibility

  • Main dashboard: company vs. subcontractor-owned split
  • Stock by ownership donut and by-vendor bar chart
  • Per-subcontractor breakdown of quantity and value

Aging & Risk

  • Aging report with configurable buckets
  • Value-at-risk report for finance/insurance
  • Drill-down into underlying stock.quant / stock.move.line records

Alerts & Export

  • Optional overdue alert (activity or chatter message) per vendor
  • XLSX export on every report screen
  • Configurable aging thresholds and alert settings
