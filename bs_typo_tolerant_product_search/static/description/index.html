Typo-Tolerant Product Search

Multi-word fuzzy fallback, configurable sensitivity, and an automated search-miss log for eCommerce product search — never lose a sale to customer spelling errors.

Feature overview

Delivers multi-word typo tolerance, search-miss intelligence, and sensitivity controls directly into your Odoo eCommerce storefront.

Multi-Word Fuzzy Matching

  • Word-by-word fuzzy correction when exact storefront search yields zero results
  • Resolves native Odoo limitation that skips fuzzy correction on multi-word phrases
  • Displays clear "Showing results for..." suggestion notice for shoppers
  • Preserves SKUs and serials with built-in alphanumeric ratio guards
  • Prevents zero-result drop-offs on common customer typos and misspellings

Configurable Sensitivity

  • Three distinct sensitivity levels: Tight, Normal, and Loose
  • Tight mode requires close spelling similarity for high-confidence suggestions
  • Loose mode maximizes product discovery across large product catalogs
  • Configurable minimum result threshold before fuzzy fallback engages
  • Fully compatible with PostgreSQL trigram (pg_trgm) indexing

Search Miss & Conversion Log

  • Dedicated log view at Website → Reporting → Search Miss Log
  • Records raw search queries alongside corrected term suggestions
  • Tracks the number of results returned for each corrected search
  • Monitors visitor click-through conversion on suggested products
  • Uncovers product catalog naming gaps and frequent customer queries
